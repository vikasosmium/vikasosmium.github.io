Places I've Explored

🏛️ Taj Mahal, Agra

One of the Seven Wonders of the World and a symbol of love. Visiting the Taj Mahal was an unforgettable experience.

🏔️ Bir

Famous for paragliding, breathtaking mountain views, and peaceful monasteries.

🌄 Baijnath

Home to the historic Baijnath Temple and surrounded by beautiful Himalayan scenery.

🌿 Palampur

A beautiful hill station known for its tea gardens and stunning mountain views.

🌊 Udaipur

The City of Lakes, known for its majestic palaces, lakes, and rich cultural heritage.

🕉️ Haridwar

A sacred city on the banks of the Ganges, famous for its spiritual atmosphere.

🧘 Rishikesh

The Yoga Capital of the World, offering spirituality, adventure, and natural beauty.
